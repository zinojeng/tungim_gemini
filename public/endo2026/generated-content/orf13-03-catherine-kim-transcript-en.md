## Background and androgen panel

11-oxygenated adrenal androgens remain elevated in later life, and this has implications for diseases of aging. As many of you in this room know, the 11-oxyandrogens include 11-ketotestosterone (11KT), 11β-hydroxytestosterone (11OHT), 11-ketoandrostenedione (11KA4), and 11β-hydroxyandrostenedione (11OHA4).

## CARDIA cohort and statistical methods

For this analysis, we used data from CARDIA. It is a large, NIH-supported, prospective longitudinal cohort study designed to assess cardiovascular disease (CVD) risk factors.

For this subanalysis, we examined 455 women who had androgen levels measured at CARDIA Exam Year 2, when they were about 27 years old, as well as Exam Year 25, when the same group of women was about 50 years old. The cohort has been in existence for 40 years, and LC–MS/MS was performed by the Auchus Laboratory at the University of Michigan.

For statistical analysis, we used generalized estimating equation (GEE) models to examine associations between 11-oxyandrogens and CVD risk factors across the reproductive lifespan.

## Cohort characteristics at Exam Years 2 and 25

These are the characteristics of our population. At the Year 2 exam, women were about 27 years old, and at the Year 25 exam, they were about 50. About half of the population was Black. About one-third used oral contraceptive pills (OCPs) at Year 2, but this dropped at Year 25. Estrogen use was low at both time points.

About one-fifth of the women had undergone gynecologic surgery, such as oophorectomy or hysterectomy, by Year 25. Women were not obese at either time point on average, although their weight increased over the lifespan. Their systolic and diastolic blood pressures increased, as did their fasting glucose. This was less true of lipid levels, except for triglycerides.

## Associations with mean arterial pressure

Androgen levels were associated with mean arterial pressure. For every 10 ng/dL of the various androgens, we see a percent change in mean arterial pressure. There was no change with testosterone. There was an association between free androgen index (FAI) and mean arterial pressure, and 11KT, 11OHA4, and 11KA4 were significantly associated with higher mean arterial pressure.

This was after adjustment for age, body mass index, race, estrogen use, and medications.

## Associations with elevated fasting glucose

When we looked at associations between 11-oxyandrogens and the odds of elevated fasting glucose, we also observed associations between the 11-oxyandrogens and elevated fasting glucose. 11OHT, 11KT, 11OHA4, and 11KA4 were all associated with higher odds of elevated fasting glucose after adjustment for other risk factors.

## Sensitivity analyses and lipid findings

We performed several sensitivity analyses. We added testosterone to the 11-oxyandrogen models, and we found that the associations persisted. We restricted the analysis to non-estrogen users, and the associations persisted. Associations were similar by race.

We did not observe consistent patterns with lipid levels, except that 11OHT was associated with lower triglycerides.

## Conclusions

We conclude that 11-oxyandrogens are associated with higher blood pressure and higher odds of elevated fasting glucose. Additional studies are needed to examine modifying factors and associations with coronary artery calcification (CAC) and CVD events. Thank you.
