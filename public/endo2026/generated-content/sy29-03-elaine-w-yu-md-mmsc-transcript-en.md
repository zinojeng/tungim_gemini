## Clinical considerations

So I just want to end with some clinical considerations. So all patients who've undergone bariatric surgery should be counseled to receive adequate calcium, and our goal intake is higher than for standard postmenopausal osteoporosis. We're really looking for 1,500 milligrams per day through a combination of dietary sources and supplements, and the supplements that you use should really be calcium citrate because carbonate is not very well absorbed in the setting of bariatric surgery. In addition, aggressive vitamin D supplementation is recommended on the order of 2,000 to 3,000 IU per day. In reality, we all just titrate to actually achieve 25-hydroxyvitamin D levels, and I recommend a level greater than 30 ng/mL in these patients.
