## Discussion and the OPTIMAL study

So now moving on to the discussion.

This case made us think about why we were finally able to localize corticotroph microadenoma that was previously occult on MRI.

So a bit of hypothesis here.

The normal pituitary gland naturally takes up methionine.

Corticotroph microadenomas, which are metabolically active, have greater methionine uptake than the normal pituitary gland.

With pharmacological optimization using osilodrostat, which blocks cortisol synthesis, the reduction in negative feedback increased ACTH and tumor activity, further enhancing methionine uptake on PET imaging.

So by comparing the baseline and post-treatment scan, what we got, a delta image can be generated, which may enhance detection and localization of corticotroph adenoma.

So this led to the development of a prospective multicenter observational study called the OPTIMAL study.
