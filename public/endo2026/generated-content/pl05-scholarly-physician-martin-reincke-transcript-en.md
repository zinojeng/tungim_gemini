## Outstanding Scholarly Physician Award

I'd like to take a few minutes now to recognize some more of our Laureate Award recipients. First up is our Outstanding Scholarly Physician Award, which recognizes outstanding contributions to the practice of clinical endocrinology in academic settings.

This year's recipient is Professor Martin Reincke, MD. Dr. Reincke is Professor of Endocrinology and Chair of Medical Department IV at Ludwig-Maximilians University Hospital in Munich, Germany, one of the leading institutions in German academic medicine.

He is an internationally recognized leader in the diagnosis and management of adrenal and pituitary disorders who played a key role in building a large-scale international research consortium to address major therapeutic challenges in patients with primary aldosteronism and Cushing syndrome. He previously served on the Editorial Board of *The Journal of Clinical Endocrinology & Metabolism*.

Please welcome to the stage Dr. Reincke.
