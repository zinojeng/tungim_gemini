### Distinguishing adrenal insufficiency from glucocorticoid withdrawal syndrome

So after surgery, we know that we have to deal with two different problems: adrenal insufficiency, which is very common with patients after surgery, and the glucocorticoid withdrawal syndrome. So the glucocorticoid withdrawal syndrome, like any endocrine withdrawal syndrome or any other withdrawal syndrome, has an onset with severe psychiatric and physical conditions that happens very early after surgery, three to ten days after surgery. And specifically in this case is characterized by myalgia, muscle weakness, and fatigue, hypersomnia among the most important clinical features. However, what's the point here is that most of those symptoms resemble that of adrenal insufficiency, and sometimes it's very difficult to distinguish the two conditions. So what we can do in clinical practice is to try to distinguish these two conditions by assessing some specific symptoms that resemble more adrenal insufficiency than glucocorticoid withdrawal syndrome.

For instance, if we have a patient after surgery that is complaining about those symptoms but also manifests hypotension or hyponatremia, hypoglycemia, or even worse, vomiting, in those cases the probability of having adrenal insufficiency is much higher than having just a glucocorticoid withdrawal syndrome. Another thing that could be really helpful in clinical practice is at which point of the tapering of glucocorticoid are we with the patients, because if we are in a supraphysiological doses, like we do in exogenous Cushing syndrome, for instance, and we have those symptoms, the probability of having adrenal insufficiency is quite low, even though we have to be very careful because we have to be sure that the patient is taking the drugs properly, that there are no intercurrent illness or there are no intercurrent gastrointestinal condition that might hamper the absorption of the drug.

So we have still to be careful, even though the probability is quite low. If we are in physiological range or under physiological range, then we have to be very careful because there the manifestation could be indicating adrenal insufficiency.
