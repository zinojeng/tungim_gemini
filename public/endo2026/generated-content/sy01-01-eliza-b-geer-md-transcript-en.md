### Definitions and clinicopathologic risk markers

The important point is that this definition is fundamentally based on clinical behavior over time.

### Integrated molecular profiling framework

I would like to finish by considering an integrated molecular profiling framework for risk stratification and treatment selection.

Clinicopathologic features remain the foundation, but they are imperfect markers.

Next-generation sequencing can detect gene-level alterations, which identify subsets of corticotroph and lactotroph tumors with more aggressive biology and worse outcomes.

And the recent ESE clinical practice guidelines for aggressive tumors suggested assessing for these pathologic and molecular features.

Copy number and LOH analysis provide broader genomic information.

Fraction of LOH and recurrent chromosomal LOH, especially in the corticotroph tumors, may identify poor prognosis tumors.

Cell cycle alterations could be a signal of metastatic potential.

Methylation profiling refines lineage-specific risk groups and may also provide treatment-relevant information through MGMT promoter methylation.

Finally, mismatch repair deficiency and temozolomide-induced hypermutation may identify tumors more likely to respond to immune checkpoint inhibition.

And importantly, no single marker is sufficient.

Risk assessment requires integrated clinicopathologic, genomic, epigenomic, and longitudinal profiling.

In summary, these data point towards an integrated molecular classification of aggressive pituitary tumors.

At diagnosis or initial surgery, we should still begin with careful histopathology, lineage assignment, proliferative markers, and assessment of invasion.

But for tumors with high-risk features, molecular profiling may add clinically meaningful information.

At progression, repeat sequencing may be especially important.

Tumors evolve over time, and acquired alterations such as cell cycle pathway changes, mismatch repair deficiency, or temozolomide-induced hypermutation may change both prognosis and treatment options.

The next step is prospective validation in multicenter cohorts, followed by integration into surveillance and treatment selection algorithms.

And the overall goal is to move from a retrospective recognition of aggressive behavior to earlier risk stratification and rational treatment selection.
