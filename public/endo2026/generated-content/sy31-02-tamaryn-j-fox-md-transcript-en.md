## Learning points

The main learning points for this case are, one, this is the occurrence of two rare hormone-producing tumors in the same patient, and a dopamine-secreting paraganglioma can indeed result in inhibition of pituitary prolactin secretion.

Immature PIT1-lineage pituitary adenomas are less well differentiated, can have variable hormonal staining and secretion, and tend to be larger and more aggressive.

Dopamine-secreting paragangliomas are often underrecognized, as they frequently lack symptoms, and overall genetic testing should always be considered in patients with pheochromocytoma or paraganglioma (PPGL) because of implications for the patient and their family.
