## Professional guidance

## Counseling conclusions

So how do we counsel patients? I'm not going to give you my sob story up here, but I'm sure it'll come to a surprise as nobody that all my funding was pulled. And so all of our studies stopped overnight. We basically got a cease and desist letter, which is why I am presenting very similar data to what I presented last year when it comes to our mice, maybe just with some new insights as I get to think without being in the lab. But there's so many nuances about that effect and the reversibility that we still need to resolve, and we need much more data. Liz Rubin out in Oregon is actually doing that in primates now, which is obviously much closer to a human model, and so I think we'll get some really interesting—and ASRM just funded her to continue—so I think we'll get some really interesting data out of there.
